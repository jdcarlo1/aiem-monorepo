**Audience:** technical buyer / diligence partner  
**Positioning:** paper research terminal with real engines (not live brokerage; OE sold separately)

- “This is **AIEM Terminal** — autonomous equity research + paper book.”
- “**Options Engine is a separate product** — not required for AIEM.”
- “Live brokerage is optional/future and hard-locked today.”

## 1:00–3:00 — Command Center
- Open `/aiem/command`
- Show system live heartbeats / SSE stream
- Point to morning jobs and health

## 3:00–5:00 — Sales Readiness (buyer proof)
- Open **Commercial → Sales Readiness**
- Tab 1: Reliability — prediction streak vs 5-day sale bar
- Tab 2: Honest P&L — null marks = 0, option entry mid coverage

## 5:00–7:00 — Morning intelligence

## 7:00–9:00 — Paper book + Performance

- Performance: win rate, Sharpe/Sortino, drawdown — with honesty caveats for options

## 9:00–11:00 — Intelligence depth
- Probability / Calibration
- Specialist Council or Pattern Lab (one deep dive)
- Risk page

## 11:00–12:00 — Close
- Re-state what is included vs not

- Next step: 5 green trading days post-Publish + diligence pack review
