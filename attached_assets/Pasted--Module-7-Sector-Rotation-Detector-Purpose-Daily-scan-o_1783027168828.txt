# Module 7: Sector Rotation Detector

## Purpose
Daily scan of sector-level price/volume data (via Polygon) to detect which sectors are heating up or cooling off — independent of any single-stock options flow. Surfaces rotation as a tiered-confidence signal (early move → developing trend → confirmed rotation) rather than a single yes/no flag, since one day of data cannot reliably distinguish real rotation from noise.

Output feeds two places:
1. **Telegram alert** — same channel as existing AIEM alerts
2. **Conviction weighting input** — sector state should down-weight new bullish signals in cooling sectors and up-weight them in heating sectors, tying directly into the Fragility & Crowding layer

## Core metrics (computed daily, end of day)

For each sector ETF:
1. **Relative strength vs. SPY** — sector return minus SPY return, over 1-day, 3-day, 5-day, 20-day windows
2. **Volume ratio** — today's volume vs. 20-day average volume
3. **Rank** — sector's position (1–12) by relative strength for each window, vs. yesterday's rank
4. **Trend inflection** — was the sector in the bottom half of rankings 5+ days ago and is now trending toward top half (or vice versa for cooling)? Simple rank-of-rank-change over time.

## Tiered confidence logic

**Tier 1 — Early move** (informational only, no alert push, logged only)
- Trigger: 1-day relative strength move > +1.5 SD from that sector's own 60-day daily move distribution, OR a rank jump of 4+ positions in 1 day
- Purpose: internal logging so you can later check "did Tier 1 flags actually turn into real trends" — this is your own backtest data on the module itself

**Tier 2 — Developing trend** (Telegram alert, marked "watch")
- Trigger: relative strength positive (or negative, for cooling) across BOTH the 3-day AND 5-day windows, with volume ratio > 1.2x average on at least 2 of the last 3 days
- This is roughly the point where the semis-selloff / financials-strength situation would have crossed into "worth watching"

**Tier 3 — Confirmed rotation** (Telegram alert, marked "confirmed," feeds conviction weighting)
- Trigger: relative strength positive/negative across 3-day, 5-day, AND 20-day windows, sustained rank in top-3 or bottom-3 for 4+ consecutive trading days, volume ratio > 1.2x average sustained
- Only Tier 3 states should actually adjust AIEM's conviction scores — Tiers 1 and 2 are visibility only, not action triggers, to avoid reacting to noise

## Guardrail: this is directional context, not a trade signal
This module does not generate buy/sell signals on its own. It answers "which sectors are structurally strong or weak right now" so existing signals get weighted accordingly. A Tier 3 "cooling" flag on semiconductors should make AIEM more skeptical of new bullish semis signals — it should not, by itself, generate a bearish trade.

## Schema

New table `aiem_sector_rotation`:
- `date`, `sector_ticker`, `spy_relative_1d`, `spy_relative_3d`, `spy_relative_5d`, `spy_relative_20d`, `volume_ratio`, `rank_today`, `rank_yesterday`, `rank_change`, `tier` (1/2/3/null), `direction` (heating/cooling)

New table `aiem_sector_alerts_log`:
- `date`, `sector_ticker`, `tier`, `direction`, `message_sent` (bool) — lets you later measure how often Tier 1/2 flags actually became Tier 3 confirmed trends, which tells you whether the thresholds are well-calibrated

## Scheduler placement
Runs daily after market close (suggest 5:00 PM ET, before your existing Sunday-only weekly modules) — this is a daily job, not weekly, since sector rotation is a faster-moving signal than your hypothesis validation pipeline.

## Integration point with Fragility & Crowding (Layer 10)
Once Tier 3 states exist, Layer 10 should query the current sector state for any ticker being scored and apply a weighting adjustment — e.g., reduce conviction score by a fixed amount for bullish signals in a Tier 3 "cooling" sector, and the inverse for "heating." Exact weighting magnitude should be tuned empirically once you have a few months of Tier 3 history to check against.

## What to watch for once live
- If Tier 2/3 alerts fire on nearly every sector every week, thresholds are too loose — tighten the SD/rank-jump cutoffs
- If Tier 1 flags rarely progress to Tier 3, that's expected and fine — it confirms most single-day moves are noise, which is the whole point of the tiering
- Track false-positive rate on Tier 3 "confirmed" calls over time using the alerts log — this is your evidence for whether the module is actually adding value to conviction scoring, not just adding noise
