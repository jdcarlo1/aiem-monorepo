# PURPOSE

The Trend Integrity Engine determines whether a stock is:

• Still in a healthy long-term uptrend

• Experiencing a normal correction

• Entering a weakening trend

• Beginning a structural breakdown

• Becoming a high-quality tactical dip-buy opportunity

This engine is one of the most important components inside AEIM.

A stock can decline sharply without losing its long-term trend.

AEIM must understand that difference.

---

# PRIMARY RULE

NEVER classify a stock as "broken"
because of one indicator.

A single moving average

A single RSI reading

One red candle

One bad day

None of these alone are sufficient.

Trend changes require MULTIPLE independent confirmations.

---

# WHY THIS MATTERS

Being above the 200-day moving average
generally indicates that the long-term trend is still intact.

Being above the 50-day moving average
suggests the intermediate trend remains healthy.

A short-term pullback while these longer-term conditions remain favorable
does not automatically imply the stock has entered a bear trend.

Instead, it may simply represent a correction within an existing uptrend.

AEIM must evaluate this using multiple pieces of evidence rather than
drawing conclusions from one measurement alone.

---

# HIGHER LOWS

One of the most important rules.

As long as higher lows remain intact,

the trend is usually still healthy.

When higher lows begin failing,

trend quality deteriorates.

---

# STRUCTURAL BREAKDOWN

A structural breakdown usually requires
multiple independent warning signs.

Examples include:

Price loses important support.

Higher lows fail repeatedly.

Intermediate moving averages weaken.

Long-term trend measures flatten or decline.

Relative strength deteriorates.

Heavy distribution appears.

Sector leadership weakens.

Institutional participation declines.

Only when several conditions align
should AEIM classify the trend as
STRUCTURAL_BREAKDOWN.

---

# LEADERSHIP

Some industries lead entire bull markets.

Others become laggards.

AEIM must monitor leadership continuously.

Leadership today
does not guarantee leadership tomorrow.

---

# EXAMPLE LOGIC

If a stock experiences a sharp short-term decline
yet still satisfies objective trend criteria such as:

• Long-term moving average remains supportive

• Intermediate trend remains constructive

• Relative strength remains favorable

• Price structure remains healthy

then AEIM should evaluate it as a possible
healthy correction rather than automatically
classifying it as a failed trend.

Conversely, if multiple deterioration signals
appear simultaneously, confidence should decrease.
