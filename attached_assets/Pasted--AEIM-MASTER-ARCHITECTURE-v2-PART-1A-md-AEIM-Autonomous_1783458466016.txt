# AEIM (Autonomous Evidence Intelligence Machine)
## Enterprise Autonomous Trading Architecture
### Part 1A — Executive Architecture & Master Decision Pipeline

Version: 2.0
Status: Engineering Specification
Target: Replit Engineering Team

---

# PURPOSE

This document defines the core architecture for AEIM's autonomous reasoning engine.

AEIM must become an evidence-driven autonomous trading intelligence system.

It should never make decisions from one indicator.

Every trade must be supported by evidence collected from every level of the market.

The goal is not simply to predict price.

The goal is to understand WHY the market is behaving the way it is.

Only after understanding the current environment should AEIM decide whether capital should be deployed.

---

# PRIMARY DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

AEIM shall behave like an experienced portfolio manager.

Every decision must answer:

• What is happening?

• Why is it happening?

• Is today's behavior normal?

• Is today's behavior unusual?

• Is this stock weak?

or

Is the entire market weak?

• Is this simply profit taking?

or

Has the entire trend changed?

Those are completely different situations.

AEIM must understand the difference.

---

# ABSOLUTE RULE

NO TRADE SHALL EVER BE APPROVED
WITHOUT COMPLETING THE ENTIRE DECISION PIPELINE.

There are NO shortcuts.

There are NO skipped modules.

Everything must execute in order.

# MACRO DOES NOT MEAN "NO BUY"

Very important.

If every index is red

AEIM should NOT automatically stop buying.

Instead ask

Are today's red conditions creating opportunities?

That question leads into the later Oversold Bounce Engine.

The macro engine is responsible for understanding the environment—not automatically rejecting every long trade.

Bad stock

Macro provides context.
