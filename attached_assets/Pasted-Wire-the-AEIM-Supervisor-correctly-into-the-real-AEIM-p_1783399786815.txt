Wire the AEIM Supervisor correctly into the real AEIM pipeline.

Important:
Do NOT build the Supervisor as a standalone report.
Do NOT make it independent from the real AEIM flow.
It must be connected directly to the existing AEIM closed-loop system.

Start the Supervisor in MONITOR-ONLY mode.

==================================================
SUPERVISOR WIRING POINTS
==================================================

Connect Supervisor at these exact points:

1. AFTER SCANNER ALERT → BEFORE AEIM INTAKE

When the stock scanner sends a candidate to AEIM, call:

supervisor_on_scanner_alert(
    audit_trace_id,
    ticker,
    signal_source,
    scanner_score,
    scanner_reason,
    alert_timestamp
)

Supervisor should log:
- alert received
- ticker
- source
- timestamp
- trace_id
- whether AEIM intake begins after this

==================================================

2. AFTER AEIM CANDIDATE RANKING

After AEIM ranks candidates and stores them in aiem_candidate_rankings, call:

supervisor_on_candidate_ranking(
    audit_trace_id,
    run_id,
    candidates
)

Supervisor must read from:

aiem_candidate_rankings

and verify:
- raw_score exists
- trust_mult exists
- drift_mult exists
- rl_weight exists
- final_adjusted_score exists
- accepted/rejected status exists

==================================================

3. AFTER AEIM FINAL DECISION

After AEIM makes final decision, call:

supervisor_on_final_decision(
    audit_trace_id,
    ticker,
    trade_id,
    decision,
    confidence_score,
    decision_reason
)

Supervisor must verify:

decision_authority = 'AEIM'

Then write:

supervisor_checked = true
meta_authority = 'AEIM_SUPERVISOR'
supervisor_verdict = 'ALLOW_MONITOR_ONLY'

No blocking yet.

==================================================

4. AFTER PAPER TRADE IS CREATED

When AEIM opens a paper trade, call:

supervisor_on_paper_trade_opened(
    audit_trace_id,
    trade_id,
    ticker,
    signal_source,
    entry_price,
    entry_time
)

Supervisor must verify:
- trade has audit_trace_id
- trade_id links to same trace
- signal_source is preserved
- trade was opened after AEIM decision

==================================================

5. AFTER PAPER TRADE CLOSES / MTM EXIT

When trade closes and pnl is known, call:

supervisor_on_trade_closed(
    audit_trace_id,
    trade_id,
    ticker,
    exit_price,
    exit_time,
    pnl,
    pnl_pct
)

Supervisor must verify:
- exit exists
- pnl exists
- pnl_pct exists
- outcome classification exists
- learning update is expected next

==================================================

6. AFTER AEIM LEARNING UPDATE

After AEIM applies learning update, call:

supervisor_on_learning_update(
    audit_trace_id,
    trade_id,
    ticker,
    signal_source,
    old_trust_score,
    new_trust_score,
    delta,
    reason
)

Supervisor must check:
- Was the update too aggressive?
- Was sample size too small?
- Did AEIM overreact to one trade?
- Is the update supported by history?
- Should this be allowed, limited, or flagged?

For now, monitor-only actions:

ALLOW_UPDATE_MONITOR_ONLY
FLAG_BAD_LEARNING_MONITOR_ONLY
REQUIRE_MORE_DATA_MONITOR_ONLY

Do not block or reverse updates yet.

==================================================
MONITOR-ONLY MODE
==================================================

Add config:

AEIM_SUPERVISOR_MODE = "MONITOR_ONLY"

Allowed values:

MONITOR_ONLY
SOFT_OVERRIDE
HARD_OVERRIDE

For now use only:

MONITOR_ONLY

In MONITOR_ONLY mode:
- Supervisor may log
- Supervisor may flag
- Supervisor may recommend
- Supervisor may grade
- Supervisor may NOT block trades
- Supervisor may NOT override AEIM
- Supervisor may NOT change trust weights directly
