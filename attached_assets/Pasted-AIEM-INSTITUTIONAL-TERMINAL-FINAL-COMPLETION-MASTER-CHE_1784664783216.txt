1. REQUIRED STATUS DEFINITIONS

Use only:

PASS:
Fully implemented and proven with current, reproducible evidence from the designated environment.

PARTIAL:
Some required behavior is implemented or verified, but at least one material acceptance condition remains incomplete.

FAIL:
Implementation exists but produces an incorrect result, violates the requirement, or fails verification.

NOT IMPLEMENTED:
No qualifying implementation exists.

BLOCKED:
Verification cannot be completed because of a specifically identified external or environmental dependency.
