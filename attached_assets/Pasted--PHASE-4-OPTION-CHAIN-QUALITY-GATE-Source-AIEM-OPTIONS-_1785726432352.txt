# PHASE 4 — OPTION-CHAIN QUALITY GATE
Source: AIEM_OPTIONS_AUTONOMY_MASTER_DIRECTIVE.txt §6
Target: aiem_strat_engine/chain_data.py, eligibility.py
Depends on: Phase 3 CLOSED
Scope: Options Engine only. Paper trading only.

## Requirements
Hard reject entire strategy when ANY leg fails: quote missing/stale/crossed,
OI < 50, option volume < 20, spread > 20% of midpoint, invalid Greeks,
invalid DTE/IV, incomplete chain.

Preferred thresholds (not hard reject, used in scoring): OI >= 500,
volume >= 100, spread <= 10%. All thresholds configurable, not hardcoded
inline without a named config source.

Compute and persist: liquidity_score, expected_slippage, fill_probability,
exit_liquidity, quote_age, chain_completeness.

Tradier remains primary chain source unless existing failover is verified
live. Polygon options module may be fallback only with freshness/source
logging on every fallback use.

## Evidence required (master §14 items 13, 14)
- Item 13: one real strategy where every leg passes — raw liquidity_score,
  expected_slippage, fill_probability, exit_liquidity, chain_completeness
  values shown, traced to the live chain data they came from.
- Item 14: one real or forced illiquid-leg case — raw proof the whole
  strategy is hard-rejected, with the specific failing threshold named.
- Grep proof the hard-reject thresholds are read from config, not inlined
  as bare literals.

## Out of scope this phase
Scoring/strategy mapping, triggers, autonomy cadence, position management —
Phases 5-8.
