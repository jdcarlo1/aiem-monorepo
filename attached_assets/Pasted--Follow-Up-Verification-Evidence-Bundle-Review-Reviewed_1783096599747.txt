2. Show where in that chain the two implementations first diverge, at what decimal place, and by what mechanism (not just "floating point" as a genre — the actual operation order difference).
3. If it turns out the divergence is NOT explainable by float non-associativity, treat it as an open formula bug and report it as such — do not re-close this item without that chain.

**Standard:** terminal output of the reordered re-run, not an assertion that "the formula is simple enough that this doesn't matter."
