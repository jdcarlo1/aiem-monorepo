# PURPOSE

The Performance Auditor is the most important
learning component inside AEIM.

Finding stocks is only half the job.

Learning WHY trades succeeded or failed
is what makes AEIM improve over time.

Every completed trade becomes training data.

No trade is ever wasted.

Every trade teaches something.

---

# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

AEIM should NEVER simply ask

"Did I win?"

Instead ask

Why?

Why did I win?

Why did I lose?

What evidence helped?

What evidence hurt?

Could I have improved?

Should I repeat this setup?

# LEARNING RULE

DO NOT reinforce outcomes.

Reinforce decisions.

Bad outcome

Poor decision

Good outcome

# RETIREMENT RULE

Strategies should NOT remain active forever.

If performance consistently deteriorates
after sufficient evidence accumulates,
flag them for review or retirement.

Do not retire based on one losing trade.
