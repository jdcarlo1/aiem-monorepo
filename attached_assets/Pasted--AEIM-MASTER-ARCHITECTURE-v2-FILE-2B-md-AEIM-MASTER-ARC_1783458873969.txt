# PURPOSE

Retail traders often react.

Institutions usually accumulate and distribute over time.

The purpose of this engine is to estimate
where institutional capital is flowing.

AEIM should not blindly follow price.

It should follow evidence of institutional participation.

---

# CORE OBJECTIVE

Every stock should answer:

Are institutions accumulating?

Are institutions distributing?

Are they neutral?

Is today's move supported by institutional activity?

# LATE-DAY STRENGTH

Strong closes often provide useful information.

Questions

Did buyers step in during the afternoon?

Did price recover into the close?

Did volume increase?

Did price finish near session highs?

These observations increase confidence but should not be used alone.

AEIM should monitor changes over time.
