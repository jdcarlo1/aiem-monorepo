# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

The Macro Context Engine is the very first reasoning layer inside AEIM.

Its purpose is NOT to pick stocks.

Its purpose is to determine whether today's environment is favorable, neutral, or hostile before any stock is analyzed.

AEIM should think exactly like a professional portfolio manager.

Before asking:

"Should I buy this stock?"

It must first ask:

"What kind of market am I trading today?"

Without answering that question first, every stock decision is incomplete.

# INDEX ANALYSIS

AEIM shall always understand:

Is the S&P strong?

Is Technology strong?

Are Industrials strong?

Are Small Caps participating?

Examples:

Scenario A

SPY +0.8

QQQ +1.4

IWM +0.9

DIA +0.5

Interpretation

Healthy participation.

Risk appetite exists.

Long exposure can remain normal.

---

Scenario B

SPY -0.6

QQQ +1.1

IWM -2.0

DIA -1.0

Interpretation

Money is concentrating into large-cap technology.

Small caps are weak.

Institutional participation is narrowing.

Risk should be reduced.

---

Scenario C

SPY -2.2

QQQ -2.8

DIA -2.1

IWM -3.5

Interpretation

Broad liquidation.

Do NOT automatically reject every long trade.

Instead:

Increase evidence requirements.

Reduce size.

Search for exceptional opportunities only.

# PANIC DETECTION

Indicators include:

Large VIX expansion

Broad index declines

Heavy downside volume

Large breadth deterioration

Credit spread expansion

Rapid Treasury movement

Unexpected geopolitical events

Central bank surprises

If several occur together:

Market Survival Engine should raise risk level.
