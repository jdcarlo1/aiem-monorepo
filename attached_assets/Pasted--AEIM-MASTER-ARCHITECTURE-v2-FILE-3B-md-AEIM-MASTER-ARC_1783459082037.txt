# PURPOSE

The purpose of the Reinforcement Learning Framework
is NOT to simply reward winning trades.

Its purpose is to improve AEIM's decision-making.

Winning and learning are not always the same.

A profitable trade can still be a poor decision.

A losing trade can still be an excellent decision.

AEIM must learn the quality of the decision,
not just the financial outcome.

---

# PRIMARY LEARNING OBJECTIVE

Every completed trade should answer:

Was my reasoning correct?

Was my timing correct?

Was my execution correct?

Was my risk appropriate?

Was Macro responsible?

Was Sector responsible?

Was Leadership responsible?

Would I make this same trade again?

# PENALTY ENGINE

AEIM should penalize:

Ignoring evidence

Repeated execution mistakes

Poor risk management

Entering before confirmation when history
shows waiting improves outcomes

Ignoring major macro deterioration

Ignoring structural breakdowns

Repeated overconfidence

# DO NOT PENALIZE

Trades that failed solely because of:

Unexpected macro shocks

Broad market liquidation

Temporary panic

Sector-wide selling

High-quality setups overwhelmed by
systemic market events

These should instead update
risk management rules.

---

# LEARNING RULES

Never change weights
after one trade.

Never change weights
after one day.

Require statistically meaningful evidence
before adjusting the architecture.

This prevents overfitting.

# OVERFITTING PROTECTION

AEIM must avoid learning
from noise.

Rules:

Require repeated observations.

Require multiple confirmations.

Require statistically meaningful evidence.

Avoid reacting to isolated events.

---

# DECISION CONSISTENCY

If two nearly identical situations occur,
AEIM should produce similar reasoning
unless new evidence clearly justifies
a different conclusion.

Large inconsistencies should be flagged
for review.
