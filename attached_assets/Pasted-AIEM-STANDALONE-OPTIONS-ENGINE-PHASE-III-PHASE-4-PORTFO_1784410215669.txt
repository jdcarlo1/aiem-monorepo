AIEM STANDALONE OPTIONS ENGINE — PHASE III, PHASE 4
PORTFOLIO & OPERATIONAL LEARNING
============================================================
Standalone Options Engine only. Do not modify/connect to D1-D3.
No mocks. No fabricated evidence. No manual DB inserts. Paper trading only.
Requires Phases 1-3 verified and closed. DO NOT start Phase 5 until this
phase is independently verified.

15. PORTFOLIO LEARNING
Connect directly to Phase II Portfolio Optimization and Portfolio Risk
Engine. Learn from: position sizing, total capital allocation, cash
utilization, BP utilization, reserved capital, ticker/sector/industry/
strategy/expiration/strike concentration, volatility exposure, portfolio
Greeks, correlation, diversification, drawdown, daily P/L, risk-adjusted
return, blocked vs accepted recommendations, competing uses of capital.
Optimize the whole portfolio, not isolated trade win rate. A trade that
profits while violating portfolio objectives must not be learned as
acceptable.

16. NO-TRADE LEARNING
Treat No Trade as a first-class decision. Per No Trade: store all
candidates considered, exact rejection reasons, point-in-time market
snapshot, track subsequent underlying/options outcomes, determine whether
avoiding the trade preserved capital or rejected a valid opportunity,
attribute rejection to responsible rules, measure false-rejection and
correct-rejection rates. Include No Trade performance in calibration/
learning reports.

17. OPERATIONAL & SYSTEM-FAILURE LEARNING
Learn from non-market failures: missed/delayed scans, Polygon outages,
missing chains, stale quotes, partial data, rate limits, worker crashes,
scheduler failures, DB failures, duplicate processing, stale claims, queue
failures, execution failures, alert failures, verification-chain failures.
Operational failures must generate incidents/remediation recommendations
and must never be mislabeled as trading-model errors.

STRICT VERIFICATION FOR THIS PHASE
- Portfolio-limit prevention test: a profitable trade that violates
  portfolio objectives is proven NOT learned as acceptable.
- False-rejection / correct-rejection rate calculation proven against real
  No Trade history, not narrative claim.
- Operational-vs-model-error mislabeling test: at least one real
  operational failure proven correctly classified as operational.
- Full standing evidence checklist applies.
