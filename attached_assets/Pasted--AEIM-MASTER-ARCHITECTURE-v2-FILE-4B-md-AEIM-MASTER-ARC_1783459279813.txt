# PURPOSE

The purpose of the Strategy Council is to prevent
AEIM from becoming biased toward a single way of thinking.

Instead of allowing one strategy to dominate,
multiple independent strategy specialists evaluate
the opportunity from different perspectives.

The Master Decision Engine listens to every specialist.

No specialist has final authority.

---

# STRATEGY COUNCIL

Each specialist represents a different
investment philosophy.

Examples

Trend Following Specialist

Momentum Specialist

Oversold Bounce Specialist

Breakout Specialist

Mean Reversion Specialist

Sector Rotation Specialist

Risk Management Specialist

Macro Specialist

Leadership Specialist

Portfolio Manager

Execution Specialist

Performance Auditor

---

# IMPORTANT RULE

These are logical reasoning modules.

They are NOT independent LLMs.

They are deterministic evaluation engines
operating from the same market data.

---

# RESPONSIBILITY

Each specialist answers only one question.

Example

Trend Specialist

"Is the long-term trend healthy?"

Momentum Specialist

"Is momentum improving?"

Macro Specialist

"Is today's environment supportive?"

Risk Specialist

"Is the risk acceptable?"

Execution Specialist

"Can this trade be executed efficiently?"

---
