## 1. The Bug (confirmed root cause)

`execute_stage()` opens its own `psycopg2` connection, separate from the `_c` connection used for the `aiem_paper_trades` INSERT.

- Stage 14 fires at `main.py:L40323`, writing `payload_json` (raw_score, trust_mult, drift_mult, final_score, thompson_sampled_score, thompson_multiplier, thompson_signal_source).
- The trade INSERT fires later at `main.py:L40370`.
- At the moment Stage 14 writes, the trade row does not exist yet → `paper_trade_id=None` gets permanently baked into the `aiem_diagram2_trace_audit` row for that stage.
- Stage 18 already had this exact problem and was fixed by moving it to execute *after* the INSERT. Stage 14 was never given the same fix — that's the gap.

**Consequence:** every `scoring_synthesis` audit row is orphaned (`paper_trade_id IS NULL`), and the Thompson sampling diagnostics (`thompson_sampled_score`, `thompson_multiplier`, `thompson_signal_source`) were never persisted onto the `aiem_paper_trades` row itself — only into the disconnected audit payload.

1. `UPDATE aiem_diagram2_trace_audit SET paper_trade_id = %s WHERE trace_id = %s AND stage_order = 14`

**Note the failure mode:** the except block is silent-non-fatal by design. That means if this patch throws, the system keeps running with the bug unfixed and prints an error line only — it will NOT halt trading. You must positively confirm success lines, not just absence of error lines.
