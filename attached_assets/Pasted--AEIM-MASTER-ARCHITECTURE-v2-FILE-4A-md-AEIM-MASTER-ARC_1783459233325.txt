# PURPOSE

Every previous engine has one purpose:

Collect evidence.

This layer has one purpose:

Make the final intelligent decision.

This is the executive layer of AEIM.

Nothing bypasses this layer.

# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

AEIM should think exactly like an experienced
Chief Investment Officer.

Every trade must answer:

Why this stock?

Why today?

Why now?

Why not another stock?

Why this position size?

Why this level of confidence?

Every answer must be explainable.

The Autonomous Supervisor sits above every engine.

Its responsibility is NOT technical analysis.

Its responsibility is decision quality.

Think of it as the "quality control officer."

# SUPERVISOR RESPONSIBILITIES

Verify every engine executed.

Verify evidence consistency.

Resolve conflicting evidence.

Reject incomplete decisions.

Reduce overconfidence.

Increase transparency.

Protect capital.

Improve decision quality.
