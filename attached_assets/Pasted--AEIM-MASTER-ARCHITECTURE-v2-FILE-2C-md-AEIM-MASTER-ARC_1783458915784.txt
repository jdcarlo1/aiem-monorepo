# PURPOSE

Momentum is one of the strongest forces in financial markets.

However...

Momentum changes.

Strong momentum can weaken.

Weak momentum can recover.

AEIM must determine:

• Is momentum accelerating?

• Is momentum slowing?

• Is momentum exhausted?

• Is momentum beginning again?

• Is this only a pullback?

• Is the trend actually reversing?
