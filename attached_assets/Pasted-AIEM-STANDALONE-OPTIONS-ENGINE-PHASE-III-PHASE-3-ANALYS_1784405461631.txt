AIEM STANDALONE OPTIONS ENGINE — PHASE III, PHASE 3
ANALYSIS & ATTRIBUTION
============================================================
Standalone Options Engine only. Do not modify/connect to D1-D3.
No mocks. No fabricated evidence. No manual DB inserts. Paper trading only.
Requires Phase 1 and Phase 2 verified and closed. DO NOT start Phase 4
until this phase is independently verified.

9. ROOT-CAUSE ANALYSIS
Deterministic root-cause analysis after each closed trade, expired
recommendation, blocked decision, No Trade evaluation. Evaluate: direction/
magnitude/timing correct-or-not, entry/exit too early/late, incorrect
strike/expiration/width/strategy family/position size/probability estimate/
volatility estimate, IV crush, vol expansion, theta decay, gamma exposure,
liquidity deterioration, excessive spread, slippage, fill delay, assignment/
dividend risk, regime change/misclassification, pattern failure, indicator
failure, conflicting signals, sector/market reversal, macro/news event,
portfolio concentration, correlation shock, data-quality failure, stale
data, scheduler/worker/execution/risk-rule/exit-rule failure.
Decision quality evaluated separately from financial outcome — a loss is
not automatically a bad decision; a profit is not automatically a good one.

10. INDICATOR & PATTERN ATTRIBUTION
Measure contribution of every indicator, pattern, timeframe, subsystem,
model, rule, strategy, options metric, portfolio constraint, risk control,
entry/exit rule. Methods: conditional outcome analysis, ablation testing,
permutation importance, SHAP where compatible, calibration analysis,
information coefficient, precision/recall, false-positive/negative rate,
Brier score, log loss, expected calibration error, regime-conditioned
performance, interaction-effect analysis.
No causal claims from correlation alone. Store attribution uncertainty +
sample size.

11. COMBINATION & INTERACTION LEARNING
Analyze interactions among indicators, patterns, timeframes, options
metrics, strategies, market/vol regimes, sectors, industries, liquidity
conditions, portfolio conditions, entry/exit timing (e.g. pattern+volume
confirmation, pattern+VWAP, momentum+vol regime, skew+term structure,
IV rank+strategy family, gamma exposure+expiration).
Apply false-discovery-rate control before accepting any discovered
relationship.

12. STRATEGY SCORECARDS
Separate verified scorecard per strategy/configuration: observations,
executed trades, win/loss/breakeven rate, average/median return, EV,
profit factor, Sharpe, Sortino, max drawdown, avg MFE/MAE, avg holding
time, capital efficiency, return on risk, calibration, fill rate,
slippage, liquidity failures, assignment events, performance by ticker/
sector/industry/regime/vol-regime/DTE/strike/time-of-day/day-of-week/
exit-method/portfolio-state.
Do not aggregate materially different strategy structures into a
misleading combined score.

13. SUCCESS/FAILURE KNOWLEDGE BASES
Permanent versioned DBs: successful decisions, failed decisions,
successful No Trade decisions, missed opportunities, operational failures,
data-quality failures, verification failures.
Fingerprint by market conditions, indicator/pattern combinations, options
metrics, strategy structure, regime, liquidity, Greeks, portfolio state,
risk conditions, entry/exit timing.
Repeated failures reduce confidence only after statistical validation;
repeated successes raise confidence only after out-of-sample validation.

14. MARKET & VOLATILITY REGIME LEARNING
Learn best-performing indicators/patterns/strategies per regime: strong/
weak bull, strong/weak bear, sideways, trending, mean-reverting, high/low/
rising/falling vol, risk-on/off, rising/falling rate, high-correlation/
correlation-breakdown, event-driven, earnings periods, macro-announcement
periods. Regime-specific learning must not overwrite global behavior
without verification.

STRICT VERIFICATION FOR THIS PHASE
- Attribution method proof against real closed-trade/decision data (no
  synthetic/mock sample).
- Multiple-testing correction proven applied before any relationship is
  accepted.
- Scorecard aggregation-boundary proof (dissimilar structures not merged).
- Full standing evidence checklist applies.
