   LIVE_PER_CANDIDATE
   LIVE_SCHEDULED

   RESEARCH_ON_DEMAND

    - its file exists,

    - the component executed,
    - it received the correct upstream input,
    - it produced a real output,
    - a named downstream consumer consumed that output,
    - and the complete event was persisted with trace/provenance.

13. Do not modify production until a restorable baseline has been created.
