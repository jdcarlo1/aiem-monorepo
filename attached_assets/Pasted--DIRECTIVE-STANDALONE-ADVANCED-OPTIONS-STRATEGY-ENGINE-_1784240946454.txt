# DIRECTIVE — STANDALONE ADVANCED OPTIONS STRATEGY ENGINE

SCOPE:
This is a standalone project. Do NOT connect it to Diagrams 1–3. Do NOT modify the existing AEIM pipeline or existing options-indicator intelligence. Paper trading only until separately approved and verified.

GOAL:
For every eligible stock and market thesis, generate and compare every valid options strategy, then select the structure with the best combination of:
- Capital preservation
- Defined maximum loss
- Probability of profit
- Expected value after realistic costs
- Capital efficiency
- Liquidity and fill quality
- Market-regime fit
- Volatility-regime fit
- Event-risk fit
- Portfolio-risk fit

NO_TRADE must always be included as a valid final decision.

GENERIC MULTI-LEG BUILDER:
Do not rely only on hard-coded named strategies.

Build a generic constructor supporting:
- 1–8 option legs
- Calls and puts
- Long and short legs
- Same or different strikes
- Same or different expirations
- Integer ratios
- Optional stock leg
- Debit or credit entry
- Symmetric/asymmetric wings

Requirements:
- Normalize and sort legs canonically
- Detect named-strategy duplicates
- Assign recognized strategy name when possible
- Otherwise classify as CUSTOM_MULTI_LEG
- Generate deterministic strategy fingerprint
- Reject invalid, redundant, unpriceable, or unsafe combinations
- Prevent combinatorial explosion using thesis, DTE, IV, liquidity, expected move, and risk constraints

ELIGIBILITY FILTER:
Reject any strategy with:
- Missing/stale/crossed quotes
- Missing legs
- Invalid symbols, strikes, or expirations
- Insufficient volume/open interest
- Excessive bid/ask width
- Unreliable Greeks or IV
- Unacceptable assignment or pin risk
- Unknown maximum loss
- Unknown buying-power requirement
- Undefined autonomous risk
- Excessive event exposure
- Excessive portfolio risk
- Unrealistic multi-leg fill assumptions

STRATEGY MODELING:
For every eligible strategy calculate and save:
- Net debit/credit
- Mid and conservative executable price
- Slippage
- Commissions/fees
- Maximum profit/loss
- All breakevens
- Probability of profit
- Probability of touch
- Probability of max profit/loss
- Expected value before/after costs
- Return on capital
- Return on maximum risk
- Buying power
- Capital at risk
- Reward/risk
- Delta, gamma, theta, vega, rho
- Charm, vanna, vomma/volga, speed, color where available
- IV rank/percentile
- Realized vs implied volatility
- Skew and term-structure exposure
- Expected-move coverage
- Assignment, pin, dividend, gap, and event risk
- Liquidity/fill-quality score
- Stress losses
- Payoff across price, time, and volatility scenarios

Do not use delta alone as probability of profit. Use calibrated distribution modeling, volatility surface, skew, term structure, expected move, and fat-tail stress scenarios.

RANKING:
Use a transparent Capital Compounding Score based on:
- Probability of profit
- Expected value after costs
- Capital preservation
- Defined-risk quality
- Capital efficiency
- Liquidity
- Thesis fit
- Market-regime fit
- Volatility-regime fit
- Diversification value

Penalize:
- Maximum loss
- Drawdown
- Tail risk
- Assignment risk
- Event risk
- Slippage
- Complexity
- Correlation concentration

Store every score component and weight separately.

SELECTION RULES:
1. Reject invalid/unpriceable structures
2. Reject prohibited or undefined-risk structures
3. Reject excessive capital-at-risk
4. Reject negative expected value after costs
5. Reject poor liquidity
6. Rank all remaining strategies
7. Prefer defined risk
8. Prefer lower drawdown/tail risk
9. Prefer better expected return per dollar at risk
10. Select the strongest capital-compounding strategy
11. Select NO_TRADE if no strategy clears thresholds

The selected strategy must also beat:
- Long stock
- Short stock comparison
- Long call
- Long put
- Simplest applicable vertical spread
- Cash/NO_TRADE

Do not choose a complex structure unless it materially improves capital preservation or risk-adjusted expected value over a simpler structure.

Never save only the strategy name. Every leg must be reconstructable.
