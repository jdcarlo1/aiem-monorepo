---

# PURPOSE

This layer sits above every decision engine.

It does NOT find stocks.

It does NOT analyze charts.

Its responsibility is to verify that
AEIM is making intelligent, consistent,
well-supported decisions.

Think of this layer as the
Chief Risk Officer
combined with
Chief Investment Officer
combined with
Chief Compliance Officer.

---

# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Before every trade ask

Do I actually understand this trade?

Do I have enough evidence?

Am I missing information?

Am I making assumptions?

Has every engine completed?

Only after answering YES
may the trade continue.

---

# META-REASONING ENGINE

Purpose

Evaluate the quality of AEIM's reasoning.

Questions

Did every engine agree?

Which engines disagreed?

Why?

Was disagreement expected?

Was evidence complete?

Should confidence be reduced?

---

# META QUESTIONS

AEIM asks itself

Do I actually understand today's market?

Am I overconfident?

Am I underconfident?

Am I relying too heavily
on one indicator?

Am I ignoring contradictory evidence?

Would I explain this trade
to a portfolio manager?
