Build a new autonomous layer called:

AEIM_SUPERVISOR_META_REASONING_LAYER

Purpose:
This layer sits above AEIM. AEIM is the trader. The Supervisor is the risk manager, teacher, auditor, and meta-reasoning controller.

The Supervisor must not replace AEIM. It must monitor AEIM and make sure AEIM is learning correctly, not learning bad habits, not overfitting, and not taking excessive risk.

Run this Supervisor automatically:

1. After every stock alert
2. After every AEIM final decision
3. After every paper trade closes
4. Once daily after market close
5. Once weekly for a full learning-quality review

==================================================
SUPERVISOR MODULES TO BUILD
==================================================

1. LEARNING LOOP MONITOR

Verify every alert follows this chain:

scanner_alert
→ AEIM intake
→ candidate ranking
→ AEIM final decision
→ paper trade opened or rejected
→ outcome recorded
→ learning update applied
→ future behavior changed

If any step is missing, Supervisor marks the event as INCOMPLETE.

Store results in:

aeim_supervisor_loop_audit

Required fields:

id
created_at
audit_trace_id
ticker
trade_id
loop_complete true/false
missing_steps_json
supervisor_verdict
notes

2. BAD LEARNING DETECTOR

Detect when AEIM may be learning the wrong lesson.

Examples:

- AEIM reduces trust after only one unlucky loss
- AEIM increases trust after one lucky win
- AEIM overreacts to small sample sizes
- AEIM promotes a signal with weak statistical evidence
- AEIM punishes a signal that still has positive long-term expectancy
- AEIM changes weights too aggressively
- AEIM learns from market noise instead of real signal failure

Create safeguards:

minimum_sample_size
maximum_weight_change_per_trade
minimum_confidence_for_promotion
minimum_confidence_for_retirement
noise_filter_threshold
outlier_detection

- market regime risk
- high volatility risk
- low liquidity risk
- correlation with existing open trades
- repeated losses from same signal family
- too many trades from same source

5. SIGNAL RETIREMENT AND PROMOTION CONTROLLER

Supervisor decides when signals should be promoted, demoted, frozen, or retired.

Promotion requirements:

- enough sample size
- positive expectancy
- stable across regimes
- not overfit
- recent performance acceptable
- confidence calibration acceptable

Retirement requirements:

- persistent negative expectancy
- signal decay
- repeated bad outcomes
- poor regime stability
- excessive drawdown
- failed supervisor review

6. OVERFITTING PROTECTION MODULE

Supervisor must prevent AEIM from trusting signals that only worked accidentally.

Checks:

- small sample size
- too many filters
- weak out-of-sample result
- strong in-sample but weak recent performance
- signal works only on one tiny period
- performance depends on one outlier trade
- unstable across regimes
