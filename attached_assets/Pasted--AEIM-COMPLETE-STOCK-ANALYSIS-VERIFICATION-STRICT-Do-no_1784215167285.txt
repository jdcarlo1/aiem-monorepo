## 8. DATA AVAILABILITY

Do not fabricate unavailable data.

Items such as NYSE TICK, intraday A/D, TRIN, Level II, order-book imbalance, absorption, iceberg orders or dark-pool data must be marked:
- PASS only with a real supported feed and runtime evidence
- UNSUPPORTED if the current provider does not supply the required data
- PROXY only when explicitly identified as a proxy, with the exact formula and limitations

A proxy must never be labeled as the genuine indicator.
