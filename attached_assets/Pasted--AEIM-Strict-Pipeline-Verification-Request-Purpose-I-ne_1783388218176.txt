# AEIM Strict Pipeline Verification Request

## Purpose

I need strict proof that my **AEIM autonomous AI agent** is the system making and improving decisions — not my stock scanner, stock skin, dashboard, or website signal scanner.

The goal is to verify that every stock candidate sent to AEIM goes through the full adaptive discovery, decision, and learning process, and that AEIM can prove it is learning from past outcomes.

---

## Core Requirement

Please add a strict verification/audit layer that proves the following:

1. The stock scanner only sends candidates or signals.
2. AEIM receives those candidates.
3. AEIM independently processes every stock through its adaptive modules.
4. AEIM makes the final decision or recommendation.
5. AEIM records the outcome.
6. AEIM feeds that outcome back into future learning.
7. The proof must show this came from AEIM, not the stock scanner or website scanner.

---

## Required Identity Separation

Every trace must clearly label the source system:

- `source_system`: where the stock candidate came from
- `processing_system`: the system doing the analysis
- `decision_system`: the system that made the final decision
- `learning_system`: the system that stored and learned from the result

Expected result:

source_system = stock_scanner
processing_system = AEIM
decision_system = AEIM
learning_system = AEIM

If the stock scanner, website, or stock scanner UI makes the final decision, the verification must fail.

---

## Pipeline Modules to Verify

For every stock candidate, verify this complete AEIM process:

1. Signal Received From Scanner
2. AEIM Candidate Intake
3. Outcome Tracker
4. Decay & Failure Analyzer
5. Hypothesis Promotion Evaluator
6. Adversarial Critique
7. Pattern Discovery Engine
8. Rediscovery / Variation Generator
9. Feedback Loop
10. Notifications
11. Final AEIM Decision
12. Outcome Recorded
13. Learning Update Applied To Future Decisions

---

## Required Trace Fields

Every stock must receive a unique `trace_id`.

Each module must log:

- trace_id
- ticker
- timestamp
- source_system
- processing_system
- module_name
- function_name
- file_name
- input_summary
- output_summary
- execution_time_ms
- next_module_called
- decision_authority
- learning_update_created
- status (PASS / FAIL)

---

## Strict Failure Conditions

The verification must fail if:

- AEIM does not receive the candidate.
- Any required AEIM module is skipped.
- Modules execute out of order.
- Any module is dormant or disconnected.
- The scanner makes the final decision.
- A final decision is created before the AEIM pipeline completes.
- Outcome tracking is missing.
- Results do not feed back into future learning.
- There is no proof that past mistakes affect future decisions.
- The trace cannot identify the exact file and function used.
