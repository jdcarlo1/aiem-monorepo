now correctly pass, 16/16 checks + 27/27 unit tests PASS). Two gaps remain.

## Gap 1 — Stage 11 (AIEM v3 discovery) has no revalidation at all
This is the actual autonomous stock-picking engine and the one most directly tied to
the "not picking stocks automatically" concern. It is currently PASS_THROUGH with the
stated reason "module re-run not feasible at execution time."

Required: even if a full module re-run isn't feasible, add a lighter-weight check —
re-query the stored decision's confidence and decision value from the DB at execution
time and confirm they still meet the original bar (confidence>=0.42, decision in
BUY/SMALL_BUY) and haven't gone stale. If the stored decision has a timestamp, apply
a staleness check on it too. Do not leave this source fully unchecked — state
explicitly why a DB re-check specifically (not a full module re-run) is or isn't
possible before accepting PASS_THROUGH as final.

## Gap 2 — Unrecognized sources fail-open silently
Two sources not in the original 11 (fear_premium_gex, gap_down_distribution) were
found as PASS_THROUGH, plus a blanket rule that ANY unrecognized source name is
waved through with no revalidation and no log/alert.

Required:

## Evidence required
