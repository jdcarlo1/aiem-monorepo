## Requirements
Strategy selection creates PENDING_EXECUTION_PLAN, not immediate execution.
Supported triggers: VWAP reclaim/rejection, support/resistance break,
premarket high/low break, opening-range breakout, confirmed pullback,
volume confirmation, momentum confirmation, market/sector alignment,
option spread/liquidity confirmation.

Before paper fill, refresh and revalidate: spot, chain, Greeks, IV,
liquidity, expected value, max loss, portfolio limits, trigger validity,
duplicate protection. Failed revalidation => CANCELLED/NO_TRADE.

- Item 17: one real case where pre-fill revalidation catches a changed
  condition (stale quote, liquidity gone, trigger invalidated) and the
