============================================================
AEIM INSTITUTIONAL TERMINAL
SEPARATE WEBSITE + REAL-TIME DASHBOARD
MASTER DESIGN, ARCHITECTURE, IMPLEMENTATION & VERIFICATION
============================================================
OBJECTIVE
Build a completely separate, production-quality website dedicated exclusively to the AEIM intelligence and autonomous paper-trading system.
This website must remain separate from the existing stock-scanning website.
The scanner website discovers and filters stocks.
The AEIM Institutional Terminal must show:
• How AEIM evaluates markets
• How AEIM reaches decisions
• Which indicators and modules influenced each decision
• Why a recommendation was approved, rejected, reduced, delayed, or classified as No Trade
• Live and historical paper trades
• Portfolio-level risk
• Probability calibration
• Learning and performance
• Full end-to-end decision proof
• System health and operational reliability
The terminal must be designed to meet the expectations of:
• Institutional investors
• Proprietary trading desks
• Quantitative trading firms
• Hedge funds
• Family offices
• Due-diligence teams
• Potential acquirers
• Technical auditors
This must not look like a basic retail stock dashboard.
It must feel like a professional institutional trading and intelligence terminal.
Do not copy Bloomberg trademarks, branding, proprietary layouts, fonts, or exact visual identity.
Create an original AEIM visual identity inspired by professional financial terminals.
============================================================
1. SEPARATE APPLICATION REQUIREMENT
============================================================
Create the AEIM Institutional Terminal as a separate frontend application and separate deployable website.
Do not combine it with the existing stock-scanning frontend.
Recommended structure:
apps/
  aeim-terminal/
    src/
    components/
    pages/
    layouts/
    hooks/
    services/
    stores/
    types/
    charts/
    themes/
    tests/
The AEIM Terminal may consume the same verified backend data and PostgreSQL database, but browser clients must never receive unrestricted direct database access.
Required flow:
AEIM engines, schedulers and workers
        ↓
PostgreSQL and append-only audit records
        ↓
Dedicated authenticated AEIM API
        ↓
WebSocket or Server-Sent Events
        ↓
Separate AEIM Institutional Terminal
Critical financial calculations and trading decisions must remain server-side.
The frontend must visualize stored results and must not independently recreate or override authoritative AEIM calculations.
