# PURPOSE

This document defines the engineering acceptance
criteria for AEIM.

The architecture is not considered complete
until every requirement in this document
has been demonstrated and verified.

This is the final production certification layer.

====================================================================

# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

AEIM must demonstrate that it can:

• Discover opportunities independently
• Analyze the market independently
• Make consistent decisions
• Protect capital
• Learn from experience
• Detect its own failures
• Recover safely from failures
• Explain every decision
• Improve over time

These capabilities should be verified through testing rather than assumed.
