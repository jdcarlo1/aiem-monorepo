# AEIM OPTIONS ALERT — FINAL DECISION REQUIREMENTS

Keep this inside the existing AEIM. Do not create a new diagram or large separate strategy engine.

For every options candidate, the AEIM must:

1. Confirm the underlying stock direction and market regime.
2. Evaluate price action, trend, VWAP, volume, momentum, support/resistance, sector strength and market breadth.
3. Analyze eligible calls and puts across appropriate strikes and expirations.
4. Evaluate:
   - Call and put volume
   - Volume versus open interest
   - Open-interest changes
   - Unusual options activity
   - Sweep/block activity when available
   - Bid/ask spread
   - Liquidity and estimated slippage
   - Implied volatility
   - IV rank and IV percentile
   - Delta, gamma, theta and vega
   - Gamma exposure and dealer-positioning context
   - Put/call skew
   - Expected move
   - Breakeven
   - Premium at risk
   - IV-expansion and IV-crush risk

5. Compare only:
   - Best eligible long call
   - Best eligible long put
   - No trade

6. Score the call and put using:
   - Directional probability
   - Probability of reaching the target before expiration
   - Expected return
   - Maximum premium loss
   - Risk/reward
   - Liquidity
   - Slippage
   - Theta-decay risk
   - Market-regime fit
   - Technical confirmation
   - Options-flow confirmation
   - Historical performance in similar conditions

7. Apply strict risk and quality gates:
   - Reject poor liquidity
   - Reject excessive bid/ask spreads
   - Reject excessive slippage
   - Reject contracts with unsuitable expiration or theta risk
   - Reject conflicting stock and options signals
   - Reject trades below the minimum probability and expected-return thresholds
   - Enforce position-size and daily-loss limits

8. Final output must be exactly one:
   - LONG CALL
   - LONG PUT
   - NO TRADE

9. Do not force an alert. NO TRADE is required whenever neither direction clearly meets all quality, liquidity, probability and capital-preservation requirements.

10. Every alert must include:
   - Ticker
   - Direction
   - Selected contract
   - Strike and expiration
   - Entry premium range
   - Current stock price
   - Delta, gamma, theta, vega and IV
   - Volume and open interest
   - Bid/ask spread
   - Expected move
   - Breakeven
   - Maximum premium at risk
   - Probability definition and estimate
   - Expected-return estimate
   - Profit target
   - Stop or invalidation condition
   - Selected-contract score
   - Opposite-direction score
   - Exact reasons the selected direction won
   - Main risks

Do not claim completion without runtime verification showing that every required input reaches the final call-versus-put-versus-no-trade decision.
