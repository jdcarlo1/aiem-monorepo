============================================================
AEIM DIAGRAM 2 — COMPLETE INDICATOR-BY-INDICATOR WIRING AUDIT
STRICT LIVE PROVENANCE VERIFICATION
============================================================

PURPOSE

Verify that every named Diagram 2 indicator, model, research engine,
validation control, learning component, and specialist component is:

1. Present in the real production codebase.
2. Registered under its canonical owner.
3. Initialized successfully.
4. Wired to the authorized upstream component.
5. Wired to the authorized downstream component.
6. Receiving real production or paper/shadow data.
7. Executing real implementation code.
8. Producing a real output.
9. Having its output consumed by the pipeline.
10. Contributing only where architecturally authorized.
11. Producing traceable provenance.
12. Producing auditable runtime evidence.
13. Protected against bypass, silent fallback, and fabricated success.
14. Assigned to the correct execution cadence.
15. Not duplicated under conflicting implementations.

This is not a feature-building task.

Do not create replacement architecture merely to pass the audit.

Do not mock any component.

Do not fabricate runtime traces.

Do not mark a component PASS merely because a file, function, class,
database table, configuration entry, or import exists.

A component passes only when its real implementation is proven to execute
and its output is proven to reach an authorized downstream consumer.

============================================================
MANDATORY AUDIT MODES
============================================================

Classify every component as exactly one of the following:

A. PER-CANDIDATE
Executed during analysis of an individual stock candidate.

B. PER-MARKET-CYCLE
Executed once per scheduled market scan or market-state refresh, with its
output then attached to one or more candidate traces.

C. PERIODIC RESEARCH
Executed on a scheduled daily, weekly, monthly, or retraining cadence.

D. LEARNING / POST-OUTCOME
Executed only after decisions, fills, holdings, or outcomes become available.

E. GOVERNED OPTIONAL
Executed only when an explicit trigger condition is satisfied.

A component must not fail solely because it is not intended to execute on
every stock. However, its actual cadence, trigger, most recent real run,
and downstream usage must be proven.

“Not applicable” is prohibited unless the architecture explicitly excludes
the component. Any exclusion must be reported as a Diagram 2 discrepancy.
